With 47-day certificates (and Let's Encrypt's 6-day ones) this isn't a yearly accident any more. It's a weekly chance.

How the copy is checked

  1. 1
    Every hook. Resolve all A and AAAA records, or pass backends yourself with --connect 10.0.0.7:443.
  2. 2
    Every key. A TLS handshake per address with the right SNI, chain and hostname verified, served certificate kept.
  3. 3
    One gauge. Compare fingerprints across nodes, flag the odd one out, and warn when a cert is down to its last days or last third of its lifetime.
  4. 4
    A receipt. Exit code 0 / 1 / 2, JSON, or Prometheus textfile metrics for node_exporter.
